Fun Collages arranges a handful of photos from your library into a single image, in one
of two layouts: **Collage**, a free arrangement, or **Stripe**.

## Handling

- Pinch a photo to resize it.
- Rotate a photo with two fingers in collage mode.
- Tap a photo to bring it to the front.
- Set your own collage background.
- Shoot straight from the camera on iPhone and iPod touch.

Save the finished collage or share it.

## A bit of history

This is old work. It's still listed and still free, but it hasn't been maintained in a
long time — several of the sharing destinations named in the original description may no
longer work.

It did once chart in the top ten downloaded iOS photography apps in a good number of
countries. There was also, back then, considerably less to beat.
